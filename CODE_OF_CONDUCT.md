# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and do not harass anyone
or post anyone's private information. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that do not follow
this code, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately via the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub private reporting).
All reports are reviewed confidentially.
